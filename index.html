Privacy Policy — Fatura Invoice

Effective date: 25 July 2026

In short: Fatura Invoice keeps everything on your device. It has no accounts, no servers, and no tracking. It does not collect your data and never sends your invoices or client details anywhere. The only time it uses the internet is to process an optional in-app purchase through Apple.

This policy explains how the Fatura Invoice app (“the app”, “we”) handles information. It applies to the iOS app only.

The short version

  • The app stores your invoices, clients, saved services and business details only on your device.
  • We do not collect any of it. There are no accounts and no servers.
  • The app never sends your invoices, clients or business details anywhere. The only time it contacts the internet is to process an optional in-app purchase through Apple (see “Purchases” below) — your invoice data is never involved.
  • There is no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising, and no third-party software of any kind is included.

Information the app stores on your device

To create invoices, the app saves the following on your device, in the app’s private, encrypted storage:

  • Your business details (name, ABN or ACN, address, contact details, logo, payment instructions).
  • Your clients (name, phone, email, address).
  • Your saved services and your invoices.

This information never leaves your device except when you choose to share a finished invoice (see “Sharing invoices” below). We never see it.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no tracking, and no third-party software development kits.

Device features the app uses

  • Contacts. If you tap “Add from Contacts”, iOS shows its own contact picker. The app does not get access to your address book — only the single contact you choose is returned, and it is saved on your device. That is why the app never asks for contacts permission.
  • Photos. If you add a business logo, iOS shows its own photo picker. The app does not get access to your photo library — only the single image you choose is saved on your device. That is why the app never asks for photos permission.
  • Face ID / passcode. If you turn on the app lock, the app uses Apple’s local authentication to unlock. The app never receives your face data or passcode — iOS only tells it whether the unlock succeeded.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored in the app’s private storage and is encrypted while your device is locked, using Apple’s built-in data protection. You can require Face ID or your passcode to open the app.

iCloud Backup. If you have iCloud Backup switched on for your device, your device backups — which include this app’s data — are stored in your own iCloud account and encrypted by Apple. This is your device’s normal backup, controlled by you in iOS Settings, not something the app does. The app itself does not use iCloud, iCloud sync, or any other cloud service.

Sharing invoices

When you tap Share on an invoice, iOS shows its standard share sheet and you choose where the PDF goes (for example Mail, Messages, or WhatsApp). The app does not send anything on its own, and it has no say in what you do with the file after that.

Purchases

The app is free to use, with a one-time optional purchase that removes the limit on how many invoices you can share. If you make that purchase, it is handled entirely by Apple’s in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, card, or any payment details. The app only learns whether this device has unlocked the purchase — nothing else. We do not run any servers and do not receive any personal information from the purchase.

Third parties

The app includes no third-party services, no software development kits, and no advertising or analytics providers. Nothing about you is shared with anyone. Purchases are handled by Apple, as described above.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your control over your data

  • You can delete any invoice, client or saved service inside the app at any time.
  • Deleting the app from your device removes all of its data.
