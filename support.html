Common questions

  • How do I set up my business details? Open the app and follow the welcome setup, or go to Settings at any time to edit your business name, ABN or ACN, address, contact details, logo and payment instructions.
  • How do I add a client? On a new invoice, tap “Add from Contacts” to pick one from your phone, or type the details in directly.
  • How do I send an invoice? Open the invoice, tap “Preview and share”, then choose where to send the PDF (for example Mail, Messages or WhatsApp).
  • What is the one-time unlock? The app is free and lets you share a few invoices to start. A single one-time purchase removes the limit so you can send as many as you like, forever. It is not a subscription.
  • I bought the unlock on another device. Open Settings and tap “Restore purchase” while signed in with the same Apple ID.
  • Where is my data? Everything stays on your device. See the privacy policy for details.
